## A. Quick start

### What the portal is for

The Recruitment Portal keeps a hire in one place, from the moment a role is
needed to the final interview:

- You create a **role** (the job you are hiring for).
- Candidates apply, or you upload their resumes.
- **Smile**, the portal's AI assistant, reads each resume and scores it against
  the role.
- Candidates you choose do a short **AI interview**: a phone call with Smile
  (**Voice Interview**) or a video interview with Smile (**Live Avatar
  Interview**).
- You review the evidence and decide who moves on to a **face-to-face
  interview** with your team.

Smile's scores and summaries help you decide faster. They are never the decision:
a person in your team always approves or rejects.

### The main workflow in one line

**Create role → Add applicants → Smile screens resumes → You approve → AI
interview → You review → Face-to-face interview → Final decision.**

### Where to begin

1. Sign in. A new organisation sees a **Getting Started** card on the
   **Dashboard** listing the setup steps, with a button for each.
2. Make sure you have **Smile Credits** (see [Section I](#i-credits)). New
   organisations normally start with free welcome credits; you can also redeem
   a promo code or buy more.
3. Create your first role: **Role Requests → Create Role Request**.
4. Add candidates: share the role's application link, or upload resumes in
   **Resume Screening**.
5. Review applicants in **Applicants**.

Need help at any point? Select the smiling blue robot in the bottom-right corner
of any page.

## B. User types

What you see depends on your access. Menu items and buttons you cannot use are
hidden.

| User type | Who it is | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Organisation Owner** | The first person from your company to register. | Everything an HR User can do, plus invite teammates and deactivate or reactivate colleagues. |
| **HR User** | Everyone else who registers with your company email. | Create and manage roles, screen resumes, review applicants, send interviews, use the Interview Calendar, change Settings, redeem promo codes and buy credits. |
| **Credits Admin** | An account given credits-only access. | View and manage Smile Credits. No recruitment access. |
| **Role Requester** | Someone who can only request roles (if your organisation uses this). | Submit role requests and follow their own requests. Their requests go to HR for approval. |
| **Department Head** | Read-only access for one department (if used). | See their department's roles and applicants. No changes. |
| **Management** | Approval access (if used). | Approve or reject role requests and view records. Does not use HR tools. |

McLink, the portal provider, has **McLink administrators** who can manage
organisations, add credits manually and create promo codes. You will not see
these tools.

> In most client organisations everyone is an **HR User**, and the first person
> is also the **Organisation Owner**.

## E. Role Requests

A **role** is a job you are hiring for. Open **Role Requests** to see
**Submitted Requests**, with search, a status filter, sorting and **Export to
Excel**.

### Create a role

1. Select **Create Role Request**.
2. Save time: upload the job description (PDF, DOC or DOCX) or paste it into
   **Job Description**, then select **Generate draft** (or **Generate AI
   questions**). Smile fills in the screening criteria and interview questions.
   **Always read and correct what Smile fills in.**
3. Complete the **Role request** section.
4. Choose the **Interview Type** (see below).
5. Complete **Screening and Interview** (HR users).
6. Select **Create role**. The role is approved and published, and its
   application link is ready to share. Or select **Save as draft** to finish
   later. The form also saves drafts automatically while you type.

Role Requesters see **Submit for HR approval** instead; HR then approves or
rejects the request.

### Required fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Job Description** | At least 20 characters. |
| **Request Type** | Staff Addition or Staff Replacement. |
| **Employee or Position Being Replaced** | Only for Staff Replacement. |
| **Job Title** | For example "Inside Sales Specialist". |
| **Department** | Choose from the list. |
| **Employment Type** | Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, Temporary or Internship. |
| **Number of Vacancies** | 1 to 100. |
| **Target Hiring Date** | Today or later. |
| **Reason for Request** | Why the role is needed. |
| **Interview Type** | Voice Interview only, Avatar Interview only, or Both. |
| **Screening criteria** | What a strong candidate looks like (HR form). |
| **Salary visibility** | Disclosed or Not disclosed (HR form). |
| **License or certificate** | Required, Preferred or Not required (HR form). |
| **Face-to-face interview** | Required or Not required (HR form). If Required, also enter the **venue** and arrival instructions. |
| **Interview questions 1–3** | Questions Smile asks every candidate (HR form). |

### Optional fields

Salary or budget range (required only when salary is Disclosed), license
details, minimum relevant experience, keywords to look for, related experience
that may count, start-date guidance, optional scoring areas (for example
Communication quality, Culture fit), up to 3 custom scoring fields, interview
questions 4–5, and the advanced **Smile system prompt**.

### Interview Type

Choose which AI interview applicants for this role can be sent:

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Voice Interview only** | Applicants get an AI phone interview. They book a time and Smile calls them. |
| **Avatar Interview only** | Applicants get a Live Avatar video interview, which they start from the email link whenever they are ready. |
| **Both** | You choose the phone or the Live Avatar interview for each applicant. If you simply **Approve** an applicant, the candidate is offered both and chooses one. |

"Both" never means the applicant must complete two interviews.

Roles created before this option existed are set to **Both**, so they work as
before. You can change the type later on the role page (the **Interview type**
card) or with **Edit request**. Changes apply to interviews sent from then on;
invitations already sent are not affected. Only HR users can change it, and the
change is recorded in the role's **Status History**.

### Edit a role

Open the role and select **Edit request**. Change what you need and select
**Save role request** (the button stays grey until something has changed).
Screening and interview settings can also be updated on the role page in the
**Recruitment Setup** section.

### The role page

Opening a role shows:

- **Role Request Progress**: where the role is in its workflow.
- **Interview type** card: the role's interview type, with **Save** for HR.
- **Interview automation** card: optionally invite applicants to interview
  automatically when their screening score meets a minimum (for example 80%).
  It only applies to applicants screened after you switch it on.
- **Overview** and **Details**: everything entered in the request.
- **Recruitment Setup**: screening and interview settings, including the
  **Smile Avatar system prompt** used for Live Avatar interviews (with **Restore
  standard script**).
- **Applicants**: the role's applicants.
- **Status History**: every change, who made it and when.
- **Approve or Reject This Request**: for a request waiting for HR review.
  Rejecting needs a reason.

### Status, closing and removal

See [Section O](#o-status-reference) for every role status. A role stops
accepting new applicants and interviews once its **Target Hiring Date** has
passed; to keep it open, edit the date. There is no separate archive button: to
remove a role you no longer need, select **Delete request** on the role page
(this cannot be undone).

## F. Applicants

Open **Applicants** to see the **Applicant Pipeline**: every candidate in your
organisation.

### Search, filters and sorting

- **Search**: candidate name, email, role or application reference.
- **Filters**: role (**All Roles**), stage (**All Stages**), resume status
  (**Awaiting screening** / **Screened**), interview type (**Voice Interview**,
  **Live Avatar Interview**, **Not selected**) and interview status (**Not
  started**, **Scheduled**, **In progress**, **Awaiting review**, **Review
  complete**).
- **Sort**: **Newest first**, **Oldest first** or **Highest match**.
- **Clear all filters** resets everything. The refresh button reloads the list.
- Long lists are split into pages; use the page controls at the bottom.
- **Export to Excel** downloads the list.

New applicants are highlighted until you open them.

### Actions on the list

- **View**: open the applicant.
- **Edit**: change the applicant's name, email or mobile number.
- **Delete**: remove the applicant and everything about them.
- Tick several applicants to use **Approve for Interview**, **Send Phone
  Interview**, **Send Avatar Interview** or **Delete selected**. Applicants who
  are not eligible (for example, they already have an interview invitation, or
  their role does not use that interview type) are skipped, and a summary tells
  you why.

### The applicant page

- **Header**: name, application reference, current stage, and the buttons
  **View Role**, **Role Applicants**, **Edit Applicant** and **Delete
  Applicant**.
- **Summary**: role, department, date applied, **Interview Type**, **Match
  Score**, recommendation and **Next Action**.
- **AI Screening Evidence**: Smile's CV analysis (recommendation, summary,
  strengths, gaps) and the interview evidence once there is one.
- **How AI Graded This Applicant**: the score explained in plain language and
  every role setting Smile used.
- **HR Decisions**: where you approve or reject (see below).
- **Candidate Status History**: every step, who did it and when. Steps the
  portal took on its own show as **Automatic update**.
- **Status Tracking**: the four steps (AI CV Analysis, the interview, the
  interview review, Face-to-Face Interview) and their status.

### HR Decisions and notes

Each decision has a **Note** box. A note is optional when approving and
required when rejecting (a short reason). Notes are saved in the applicant's
history. There is no separate comments area; use the decision notes.

At the resume stage you can:

- **Approve**: invite the candidate to the role's interview type.
- **Send Phone Interview** / **Send Avatar Interview**: send that interview
  straight away. Only the buttons allowed by the role's interview type are
  shown. This works even if the resume has not been screened yet; screening is
  then skipped and the history says so.
- **Reject**: stop here (reason required).

For a role set to **Both**, until the candidate books or starts, **Change
interview type** lets you switch to the other interview. The old link stops
working and the candidate gets the new one by email.

After the interview, the **Voice Interview Review** or **Avatar Interview
Review** decision moves the candidate to the face-to-face interview (approve)
or stops them (reject). After the face-to-face interview, record the
**Face-to-Face Interview Decision**.

## G. Resume Screening

Open **Resume Screening**. Every resume is screened against the role you choose.

### Ways to add resumes

1. **Add Resumes for Screening**: choose a published role, add PDF, DOC or DOCX
   files (up to 10 MB each, up to the batch limit shown on the page), and start
   screening.
2. **Choose from Google Drive** (after **Connect Google Drive**).
3. **Choose from OneDrive** (after **Connect OneDrive**, if your organisation has
   it).
4. **Screen one resume**: a form for a single candidate.
5. **Send an Application Link**: email a candidate a link to apply themselves.

Candidates can also apply directly using the role's application link.

### Following progress

Each file shows **Queued** (waiting), **Processing** (being read),
**Completed/Screened** (ready to review), **Skipped** (already handled for this
role, not charged) or **Failed** (check the file and retry, not charged). Select
**View Processed Applicants** when done. **Stop after current batch** stops a
long upload.

### What the score means

The **Match Score** (0–100%) shows how well the resume fits the role's job
description, screening criteria, keywords, experience and license requirement.
Smile also gives a recommendation, a summary, strengths and gaps.

The score helps you decide who to look at first. **It is not a decision.**
Always read the evidence.

### What you can do afterwards

Open the applicant and **Approve**, **Send Phone Interview**, **Send Avatar
Interview** or **Reject**. If **Interview automation** is on for the role,
applicants who meet the minimum score are invited automatically.

Each successfully screened resume uses **1 credit**. Duplicates and failed files
are not charged.

## H. Interviews

### Voice Interview (AI phone interview)

1. The candidate receives an email with a private booking link.
2. They enter their mobile number and choose a 10-minute time.
3. At that time Smile calls them and asks the role's questions.
4. The transcript and result appear on the applicant page under **Voice
   Interview Review**.

If the candidate misses the call, use **Send Reschedule Link** on the applicant
page to send a fresh booking link.

Cost: 10 credits when completed, 8 when incomplete, 5 when there is no answer.

### Live Avatar Interview (AI video interview)

1. The candidate receives an email with a private link (no booking needed).
2. They agree to the privacy notice and recording, then pass a camera and
   microphone check.
3. Smile appears on video and asks the role's questions in order (up to 20
   minutes).
4. The recording, transcript, score and question-by-question review appear on
   the applicant page under **Live Avatar Review**.

Before your first Live Avatar interview, connect **Google Drive for Live Avatar
recordings** in Settings; without it the interview cannot start.

Cost: 2 credits per started minute (up to 40).

### Which interview is sent

This follows the role's **Interview Type** ([Section E](#e-role-requests)):

| Role setting | Approve sends | Buttons shown |
|---|---|---|
| Voice Interview only | Phone interview | Send Phone Interview |
| Avatar Interview only | Live Avatar interview | Send Avatar Interview |
| Both | A choice of both (the candidate picks) | Send Phone Interview and Send Avatar Interview |

### Results

- **Not Scored — Manual HR Review** means too little could be assessed (for
  example the interview ended early). It is not a rejection: read the
  transcript and decide.
- If the AI review failed, select **Retry analysis**.
- Recordings are deleted automatically after 90 days; transcripts and reviews
  are kept.

### Face-to-face interview and the Interview Calendar

When you approve the interview review, the candidate receives a link to book a
face-to-face interview from your team's free times (from the Google Calendar of
the role's chosen interviewer, or the shared HR calendar).

**Interview Calendar** shows all interviews by month. Select a date to see its
**Appointment Records**; select **View Applicant** to open one. Use **Today** to
return to the current month. **+ HR Exception Slot** adds an extra face-to-face
time when the calendar has none that suits. After the start time, **Mark No
Show** records a candidate who did not attend.

## I. Credits

Smile Credits pay for the AI work. Your organisation has **one shared balance**
that everyone in it uses.

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Resume screening (CV analysis) | 1 per resume |
| Voice Interview | 10 completed / 8 incomplete / 5 no answer |
| Live Avatar Interview | 2 per started minute (up to 40) |
| Creating roles, booking, notes, everything else | Free |

When the balance runs out, AI actions are paused until you add credits.

### Where to see your balance

- At the top of the menu (on every page).
- **Credits** page → **Credit Balance**: **Current balance**, **Total added**
  and **Total consumed**.

### Credit history

**Organization Credit History** lists every change: when, the event (for
example CV analysis, Voice interview, Purchase, **Promotional Credits**), the
change (+ or −), the balance after, the reference and who did it. Search it, or
filter by **Credits added** / **Credits used** and by event type.

### Redeem a promo code

1. Open **Credits**.
2. In **Credit Balance**, find **Have a promo code?**
3. Type the code and select **Redeem**.

You will see: **"Promo code applied. 210 credits have been added to your
account."** The balance updates immediately, and the history shows **+210 —
Promotional Credits** with the code as the reference.

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| This promo code is not valid. | Check the spelling. The code may also have been switched off. |
| This promo code has expired. | The promotion has ended. |
| This promo code has already been used. | All uses of this code have been taken. |
| Your organization has already redeemed this promotion. | Each organisation can use a promotion once. |

### Buy credits

On **Credits**, under **Buy Smile Credits**, choose a pack (or **Need a different
amount?** for any number from 1 to 10,000) and pay through the secure checkout.
Credits are added as soon as the payment is confirmed.

## J. Smile Bot, help and feedback

### Open Smile

Select the **smiling blue robot** in the bottom-right corner of any page. A short
"Hi! Need help?" greeting appears the first time. The robot is a small full-body
helper: it follows your cursor with its head, reacts when you click it, and
occasionally walks along the bottom of the page, saying hello when it stops. It
stands still while you point at it or while Smile is open. If you would rather it
stayed in its corner, untick **Let Smile walk around the page** on the **Help &
Feedback** tab. Select the robot again (or the
**X**, or press **Esc**) to close. With a keyboard, press **Tab** until the robot
is highlighted, then press **Enter**.

### Ask Smile

On the **Ask Smile** tab, type a question (for example "How do I create a role?")
and press **Enter**, or select one of the suggested questions. Smile answers from
the portal guide. Smile can tell you your credit balance, but cannot see
candidate records or make changes.

### Help & Feedback

On the **Help & Feedback** tab:

- **Support email**: HRSG, **hrsg@mclinkgroup.com**.
- **Copy email**: copies the address.
- **Open email app**: starts a new email to support.
- **Let Smile walk around the page**: untick to keep the robot in its corner
  (remembered on your computer).
- **Send feedback**: choose a topic (**Question**, **Report an issue** or
  **Suggestion**), type your message and select **Continue in email**. Your email
  app opens with the message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press **Send**
  in your email app.

When reporting a problem, say which page you were on, what you were trying to do
and what you saw.

## M. Alerts and notifications

### Dashboard alerts (Actionable Alerts)

| Alert | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Applicants not moving | Applicants have had no change for 5+ days. | **Review applicants** |
| Missed call interviews | Phone interviews scheduled in the past have no result. | **Open Interview Calendar** |
| Credits low or used up | AI actions may stop soon or have stopped. | **Top up credits** (or redeem a promo code) |
| The HR Google Calendar needs to be reconnected | The calendar connection has expired. | **Reconnect calendar** |
| No HR Google Calendar is connected | Needed for face-to-face interviews. | **Connect calendar** |
| Resumes could not be screened | Some files failed. | **Review resume screening** |
| Emails not delivered | Candidate emails failed to send. | **Review applicants** and check the email addresses |
| Approved roles not published | Approved roles waiting more than ~3 days. | **Review role requests** |
| Live Avatar interviews can't start: recording storage is not set up | No Google Drive folder is connected. | **Set up recording storage** |

Each alert disappears by itself once the problem is fixed.

### Other notifications

- **The bell** and the **Applicants** badge: new applicants since you last looked.
  Opening an applicant marks it as seen.
- **Credit meter** in the menu turns amber when low and red when empty.
- **Emails to candidates** go out automatically for interview invitations,
  confirmations and face-to-face bookings. The organisation owner receives a
  "new role posted" email when a role is published.

## N. Common tasks

**Create a role**
1. **Role Requests → Create Role Request**.
2. Upload or paste the job description, then **Generate draft**.
3. Check the fields, choose the **Interview Type**, then **Create role**.

**Add an applicant**
- Share the role's application link (on the role page), **or**
- **Resume Screening → Add Resumes for Screening**: choose the role, add files,
  start screening, **or**
- **Resume Screening → Screen one resume**, **or**
- **Resume Screening → Send an Application Link** to email the candidate.

**Screen an applicant**
Resumes are screened automatically when added. Open **Applicants**, filter
**Awaiting screening** / **Screened**, select **View**, read the AI Screening
Evidence and **Match Score**.

**Send a voice interview**
Open the applicant → **HR Decisions** → **Send Phone Interview** → confirm. (Or
**Approve** if the role is Voice Interview only.)

**Send an avatar interview**
Open the applicant → **HR Decisions** → **Send Avatar Interview** → confirm. (Or
**Approve** if the role is Avatar Interview only.) Make sure Live Avatar
recording storage is set up in Settings.

**Change a role's interview type**
Open the role → **Interview type** card → choose an option → **Save**.

**Redeem a promo code**
**Credits** → **Have a promo code?** → type the code → **Redeem**.

**Check credits**
Look at the top of the menu, or open **Credits** for the balance and history.

**Contact support**
Select the Smile robot → **Help & Feedback** → **Copy email** or **Open email
app**, or write to **hrsg@mclinkgroup.com**.

**Reject an applicant**
Open the applicant → type a short reason in **Note** → **Reject**.

**Invite a teammate** (owner)
**User Accounts** → **Invite a teammate** → enter their email. They join when they
register with that email.

## O. Status reference

### Role statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Still being prepared; not submitted. |
| Pending HR Discussion | Waiting for HR to review. |
| Pending Management Approval | Waiting for a management decision (older or special setups). |
| Returned for Revision / On Hold | Older statuses; these requests can still be approved or rejected. |
| Approved | Approved; setup can be completed. |
| Recruitment Setup | HR is preparing screening and interview details. |
| Job Posted | Open for candidates. |
| Rejected | Will not go ahead; see the comments. |

### Applicant stages

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Resume Review | Waiting for your decision on the resume. |
| Resume Approved | Approved; the interview invitation is being sent. |
| Interview Choice Pending | Invited; the candidate has not yet chosen a call or Live Avatar interview. |
| Voice Interview Booking Pending | The candidate can book a call time. |
| Voice Interview Scheduled | A call time is booked. |
| Voice Interview Review | The call result is ready for you. |
| Live Avatar Interview Pending | Waiting for the candidate to start the video interview. |
| Live Avatar Review | The video interview result is ready for you. |
| Approved for Face-to-Face Interview | The candidate can book a face-to-face time. |
| Face-to-Face Interview Scheduled | A face-to-face time is booked. |
| Passed Face-to-Face Interview | Passed the final step. |
| Rejected | Will not move forward. |

### Resume screening statuses

Queued · Processing · Completed / Screened · Skipped (HR skipped or duplicate; not charged) ·
Failed (not charged; retry).

### Live Avatar score bands

Strong (75+) · Good (55–74) · Partial (35–54) · Limited (under 35) · Not Scored —
Manual HR Review (too little to score; not a rejection).

## P. End-to-end workflow

1. **Create the role.** Role Requests → Create Role Request. Generate the draft
   from the job description and check it.
2. **Choose the interview type.** Voice Interview only, Avatar Interview only, or
   Both. Then **Create role**.
3. **Check you are ready.** Credits available; Google Drive recording storage
   connected (for Live Avatar); Google Calendar connected (for face-to-face).
4. **Add applicants.** Share the application link or upload resumes in Resume
   Screening.
5. **Smile screens each resume.** Watch the progress, then open **Applicants**.
6. **Review applicants.** Sort by **Highest match**, open each one, read the
   evidence.
7. **Send the interview.** **Approve**, or **Send Phone Interview** / **Send
   Avatar Interview**. Reject those who are not a fit, with a reason.
8. **The candidate does the AI interview.** They book a call or start the video
   interview from their email.
9. **Review the result.** Read the transcript, score and evidence, then approve or
   reject in **Voice Interview Review** / **Avatar Interview Review**.
10. **Face-to-face interview.** The approved candidate books a time; it appears in
    the **Interview Calendar**.
11. **Final decision.** Record the **Face-to-Face Interview Decision**.
