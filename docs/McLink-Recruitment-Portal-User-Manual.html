2 · Role requests

Create a new role request

Use a role request when your team needs an additional employee or needs to replace someone. Take a few minutes to gather the information before starting.

Before you begin

  • Know the job title and department.
  • Know whether this is a staff addition or a replacement.
  • Have a clear description of the work and the reason for the request.
  • Know the number of people needed and the target hiring date.
  • Have the salary or budget information available if required by your process.

Steps

Open Role Requests, then select “Create Role Request”.
Complete the role details.Enter the request type, department, employment type, job title, number of vacancies, and target hiring date.
Explain the need.For a replacement, name the employee or position being replaced. For any request, explain why the role is needed.
Add HR interview and screening questions if you have them.These questions help HR understand what to look for. They can be refined later.
Review and submit.The requester’s name and email are filled from the signed-in account. You cannot change those details in the form.
Tip: use the job description helperIf you have a job description file (PDF, DOC, or DOCX), upload it in the form or paste the description, then choose the option to fill in the form from it. Smile prepares role details, screening criteria, and interview questions for HR to review. Always check the suggested details before submitting.

What happens after submission?

The form autosaves changes as a draft so you can leave and return without losing work. The request is sent to HR only when you submit it, and it must be complete (job title, department, number of vacancies, reason, target hiring date, and job description) before it can be submitted or approved. The requester can follow it from Role Requests. A saved draft is not a submitted request and does not publish a job. Requesters can edit or delete their own requests, and HR reviewers can edit or delete any request; deleting asks for confirmation first.

3 · Decisions

Review and approve a role request

Open a request from the Role Requests list to read the details, comments, history, and available actions. HR reviews the request directly from the Pending HR Discussion stage — there is no separate management-approval step. Department Heads can open and read requests in their department but cannot act on them.

Finding a request

  • Use the Status filter to see requests at a particular step.
  • Search by job title, role number, department, or requester.
  • Use the sort options to find the newest request or the request with the nearest target date.
  • Select Refresh if another person may have updated the request.

Making a decision

In the HR Review panel, choose the action that matches the decision, enter a comment, and confirm. A comment is required for every role decision. Only the buttons your account is allowed to use are shown:

ButtonPermission needed

If the request has changed since you opened it, refresh the page and review it again before trying the action. If you can only view requests, no decision buttons are shown.

RememberApproval of a role is not the same as publishing a job. HR still needs to complete the recruitment setup and publish the role.
4 · Hiring setup

Prepare and publish an approved role

HR uses Recruitment Setup to tell the portal what to look for in resumes and interviews, and what Smile asks candidates. This page is available to users with Review recruitment. It can be edited while the role is Approved or in Recruitment Setup; once the role is Job Posted, it is read-only.

Complete the screening instructions

  • What should Smile listen for? Describe the evidence that matters for this role.
  • License or certificate: State whether one is required, preferred, or not needed.
  • Keywords to look for: Add useful skills, tools, or terms separated by commas.
  • Transferable skills accepted: Explain what related experience may count.
  • Minimum relevant experience: Use a simple requirement such as “None”, “3 years”, or “5+ years”.
  • Approved salary or budget range: Enter the agreed range if applicable.
  • Earliest availability instructions: Explain any start-date question to ask.

Set evaluation fields and questions

The portal always considers the candidate’s score, recommendation, strengths, and concerns. You can add optional evaluation fields and up to three custom fields (“What should Smile assess?”). Add three to five interview questions: questions 1–3 are required, and questions 4–5 are optional. If the role request supplied HR questions, they appear first and cannot be edited here. The questions are asked as written, so keep them clear, fair, and about the job.

Suggested questions may be shown for review. Treat them as a starting point, edit them as needed, and make sure the final questions are relevant to the job. Do not include questions about protected characteristics such as age, gender, nationality, religion, disability, or family status.

Use the publishing checklist

  • Choose at least one posting channel.
  • Choose salary visibility: Disclosed or Not disclosed.
  • Choose the license requirement: Required, Preferred, or Not required.
  • Choose whether a Face-to-Face interview is required. If required, enter the venue and arrival instructions.

Save and move the role forward

ButtonUse it when
SaveYou want to save changes and continue working.
Mark as Recruitment ReadyThe AI prompt, screening guidance, posting channel, and three required questions are complete.
Mark as Ready for PublishingThe publishing checklist is complete, including the face-to-face venue when required.
Publish RoleThe role becomes available in the public Open roles catalogue and for private HR application links.

If a button reports missing fields, complete the fields it lists (or choose the explicit “Not disclosed” or “Not required” option) and try again.

Good practiceRead the script preview before saving. It shows what Smile would say on a real call, using a made-up candidate, so you can catch unclear wording. Use “Advanced: edit full script” only if you need to change the whole script.
5 · Applicants

Screen and manage applicants

Open the applicant list

Choose Applicants to see the candidate list. Use the search box to find a candidate, role, email, or application number. Use the role and stage filters to narrow the list. Choose 10, 25, or 50 rows per page.

New-applicant notifications

A bell in the top bar and a badge on Applicants show how many applicants have applied since you last opened the Applicants page. HR and approvers see new applicants company-wide; a Department Head sees only their own department's. Opening the bell lists the new candidates and links straight to each record. Opening the Applicants page clears the count; a candidate who applies after that appears as new again. Users who cannot see applicants have no bell.

Pilot limitation: the “last seen” mark is stored in your browser, so the new/seen state is per browser and per device and does not follow you between devices.

Understand the summary cards

Resume Screened

Applications that have been processed and are ready for review.

Voice Interview

Candidates with activity in the phone interview stage.

Read an applicant record

Select View beside a candidate. The record brings together contact information, role, the resume, Smile's CV analysis, voice interview evidence, interview activity, decisions, and the candidate's status history. From the header you can jump to the role or to all applicants for that role. Use the resume and interview information only for recruitment purposes.

Record HR decisions

The HR Decisions panel shows the stage the candidate is at. Choose Approve or Reject and enter a comment — a comment is required every time. Decisions are made in order, and completed decisions stay visible with their comments.

DecisionWhat happens after Approve
AI CV AnalysisThe candidate is invited by email to book an AI Voice Interview.
Voice Interview ReviewThe candidate is invited by email to book a Face-to-Face Interview.
Face-to-Face Interview DecisionRecord the outcome after the meeting has taken place. Approve marks the candidate as having passed the final interview.

Users with Review recruitment or the approval permission can record decisions. Only users with Review recruitment can Edit applicant (name, email, and local phone number) or Delete applicant.

Send a personal application link

HR can invite one person to apply for one published role. The link opens the familiar candidate application page with the role and invited person’s details already prepared.

Open Resume Screening and find “Send an application link”.
Choose the published role.Only roles that are ready to accept applications can be selected.
Enter the candidate’s name and email address.Check the spelling carefully before continuing.
Choose how to send it.Select “Generate application link” and copy the link to send it yourself, or select “Generate & send email” to email it to the candidate. The email includes the required AI interview notice.
Check Applicants after the candidate submits.The link is for one successful application only and expires after a few days (7 by default). If it expires or has already been used, generate a new one.
Keep invitation links privateThe link is tied to the selected role and invited candidate. Do not post it in a group chat or send it to someone else.

5 · Resumes

Screen resumes

Users with Review recruitment screen resumes for a published role from Resume Screening. The page has three parts: Bulk upload resumes, Send an application link (section 5), and CV Analysis for a single resume. Each file is checked for duplicates, queued, and tracked on the page.

Upload several resumes from your computer

Choose the published role.Pick the role that matches every resume in this batch.
Add the files.Drag them into the box or click the box to choose them. PDF, DOC, and DOCX files are accepted. You can add up to 6 files per batch in the pilot, with a 10 MB limit per file. Each resume that screens successfully uses one Smile Credit; duplicates and files that fail before a successful analysis are not charged.
Select “Start screening”.The portal sends the resumes for processing and shows each one in the queue.
Watch the progress.The list updates automatically. You can also choose “Refresh status”.
Review the applicants.When processing is complete, choose “View Processed Applicants” to begin the human review. If some files fail, “Retry failed” resubmits only those files.

Screen a single resume

Use the CV Analysis card at the bottom of the page: choose the role, enter the candidate's details, attach the resume, and select Start CV Analysis. The candidate then appears in Applicants. It uses one Smile Credit when the analysis succeeds.

Import from Google Drive

Choose the published role first. The first time you use this, select Connect Google Drive and sign in with the Google account you want to use for your own Drive files (HR only; read-only access). After that, select Choose from Google Drive, browse My Drive or an available Shared Drive, tick the files you want (PDF, DOC, or DOCX; up to 6 per import), and confirm. Imported resumes go through the same screening pipeline, duplicate check, and one-credit-per-successful-file rule as a computer upload.

Import from OneDrive

When enabled for the deployment, OneDrive import works like Google Drive import: the same 6-file limit, 10 MB per file, accepted file types, duplicate check, and one-credit-per-successful-file rule apply. Select Connect OneDrive, sign in with the Microsoft account used for your own files (HR only), then choose Choose from OneDrive. Deployment dependent The buttons stay hidden until the Microsoft app registration, redirect URL, and administrator consent have been configured.

Queue messages

ImportantAutomatic screening is an aid for HR, not a final hiring decision. Smile compares the resume with the role's criteria and gives a score, strengths, and gaps, but a score is never an approval or rejection. Always review the resume and supporting information yourself.
6 · Smile Credits

Credits, balance, and payment

Smile Credits meter the AI features. The balance is shared by everyone in your organisation. You can always see it in the sidebar (and the mobile header), and in full on the Credits page. Booking a time, creating a role request, and commenting never cost credits.

What uses credits

ActionCredits
AI CV analysis (each resume that screens successfully)1
AI phone interview — completed10
AI phone interview — connected but incomplete8
AI phone interview — candidate did not answer5
Duplicate resume for the same role, or a failed or invalid file0 — not charged

The Credits page

The page has three parts: Buy Smile Credits (credit packs), the Credit balance (current balance, total added, total consumed, and — for authorised users — the top-up form), and the Organization credit history. Everyone in the organisation can read the history. Search it, filter by All activity / Credits added / Credits used and by event (Manual changes, CV analysis, Voice interviews), and page through it ten entries at a time. Each entry shows when it happened, the event, the change, the balance after it, a reference, and who did it.

Who can do what

AccessWhat is available
Any signed-in userView the shared balance and the organisation's credit history, and start a credit-pack purchase.
Users with Manage Smile CreditsManual top-ups Add credits with the Adjust balance form. The Admin preset has this permission; HR can grant it to anyone.

Add credits manually (demo / adjustment)

On Credits, enter a positive whole number under Adjust balance and a Note explaining why (required), then select Update Balance. Manual changes can only add credits. The adjustment is written to the credit history with your name, the amount, the note, the time, and the resulting balance, and it takes effect immediately for new AI actions. The balance is never changed without a history entry, and re-submitting the same adjustment does not add the credits twice. By default, a single top-up of 2,000 credits or more earns an automatic 10% bonus, shown as a separate “Volume bonus” entry.

Buy credits (HitPay)

Choose a credit pack.Each pack shows its credits and price in Singapore dollars. Credits are priced at S$0.40 each; by default the packs are Starter (10 credits, S$4), Standard (50 credits, S$20), and Bulk (100 credits, S$40). Your deployment may offer different packs. You only choose a pack — never a price or a credit amount.
Select “Pay with HitPay” and pay.You are taken to the secure HitPay checkout to complete payment, then returned to the Credits page.
Wait for confirmation.The page shows “Verifying your payment and credit balance…”. Credits are added only after HitPay confirms the payment to the portal directly — returning to the page is not enough on its own. This usually takes a few seconds. When it succeeds you will see “Payment Successful”, the credits added, and your new balance.
PilotPayments in the pilot run against the HitPay sandbox — no real money moves. If purchases have not been set up for the deployment, the page says “Credit purchases are currently unavailable. Please contact an administrator.”

Payment statuses

StatusMeaning
PendingPayment started, not yet confirmed. No credits added yet.
PaidConfirmed by HitPay. Credits have been added to the balance.
FailedPayment did not complete. No credits added.
Expired / CancelledThe payment link lapsed or was cancelled. No credits added.

If confirmation is slow, the page offers Check payment again, which re-checks the payment with HitPay without creating a second charge or a duplicate credit.

When the balance is too low

An AI action that the balance cannot cover is refused before any work is done, with a message showing how many credits are needed and how many remain. Top up (manually or by purchase) and try again. Existing screening and interview results are never lost.

7 · Interviews

Schedule and follow interviews

How interview times work

AI Voice InterviewFace-to-Face (HR) Interview
Who runs itSmile, by phoneHR, in person at the venue HR set for the role
Times offeredEvery day, in 10-minute times across the candidate's local dayMonday–Friday, one-hour times from 10:00 to 16:00, excluding 12:00–13:00 (lunch), in office time (Singapore)
Set up byAutomatic — nothing to configureAutomatic, and checked against the shared HR Google Calendar. HR can add exception slots.
CapacityUp to 10 calls at the same moment across all roles; a full time disappears from the listOne candidate per time; a time that conflicts with a calendar event is blocked

Candidates only see times in the current calendar month and on or before the role's target hiring date, and they never see the full internal calendar — only individual times when they book. Booking links are single-use and expire after a few days (7 by default; a settings editor can change this).

View the interview calendars

HR opens Bookings (Interview Calendars) to see the AI Voice Interview Calendar and the Face-to-Face Interview Calendar side by side, with counts of active time windows, available times, scheduled appointments, and completed interviews. Use the Role and Interview type filters, Today, and the month arrows to move around. Select a date to see open times, scheduled candidates, completed interviews, no-shows, and conflicts. Below the calendars, Appointment records lists every booking with search, a status filter, and 10, 25, or 50 rows per page; select View Applicant to jump to the candidate.

Add an HR exception slot

Standard weekday hours are already available automatically. Use an exception slot for a Saturday, a holiday, or extended hours.

  1. Choose + HR Exception Slot.
  2. Choose an approved role.
  3. Enter the date, start and end time, and timezone (Asia/Singapore, Asia/Manila, or Asia/Kuala Lumpur).
  4. Check the notice. It says whether the time is already covered by standard hours or will be added as an exception, and reports the Google Calendar check.
  5. Select Save exception slot. The slot appears on the calendar straight away.

When a candidate books

The selected time becomes a scheduled appointment, the candidate receives a confirmation, and the calendar is updated. The portal records the timezone so the candidate and HR see the same appointment time.

If a candidate does not attend

Open the date details, find the scheduled appointment, and choose Mark No Show after the interview time has started. Confirm the action. This keeps the attendance record clear and allows the team to decide whether to offer another time.

Calendar connectionFace-to-Face Interview availability is checked against the shared HR Google Calendar. If it is not connected, ask the person who manages Settings to connect or reconnect it (see section 9).

Phone (voice) interview

When HR approves a candidate at the resume stage, the candidate is emailed a secure link to book an AI Voice Interview. The candidate chooses a date and time, confirms a preferred mobile number, and is called automatically at the booked time. The times are shown in the candidate's own timezone. The email and booking page disclose that Smile, an AI interviewer, will conduct the call, that responses may be recorded, transcribed, and assessed, and that the recruitment team reviews the results. Smile's result, transcript, scores, strengths, concerns, and follow-up questions return to the applicant record.

Calls that come back as No Answer, Busy, Wrong Person, or No Show follow a retry lifecycle rather than counting as a completed interview (by default up to 3 attempts, 24 hours apart; a settings editor can change both). When a call was not answered or was incomplete, the Voice Interview Review panel shows Send reschedule link: HR chooses to send the candidate a fresh booking link, and no call is placed until the candidate books a new time.

Interview with Smile (video option)

Where enabled for the deployment, the voice-interview invitation may also include an Interview with our Avatar now button. It opens a one-time private page where Smile asks one focused question about the candidate's application on video. The response is transcribed and summarised for the recruitment team as an interview aid — it is not an automated hiring decision, and the link stops working once used or after it expires.

Face-to-face interview

After HR approves a candidate at the voice-interview stage, the candidate is emailed a new private link and books a Face-to-Face Interview against the shared HR calendar. The candidate is added as an attendee on the calendar event, and the venue or address HR entered in Recruitment Setup is included in the invitation and shown on the completed booking in the portal. After the meeting, HR records the outcome in the Face-to-Face Interview Decision panel on the applicant record.

8 · Candidate guide

For candidates: applying and booking

Apply for a role

Open the job application page.Choose a role from the public Open roles catalogue and select “View and apply”, or open the private link sent by HR. The role and your name may already be filled in.
Enter your details.Provide your name, email address, country, and mobile number.
Attach your resume.Use a readable PDF, DOC, or DOCX file and check that the file is the correct version.
Review the privacy and consent message.Continue only when you understand and agree to the stated use of your information.
Submit the application once.A confirmation message means the application was received. The role must be published and accepting applications. Sending the form again may create another application. If you were sent a private link that has already been used or has expired, the page will say so.

Book a phone interview

Open the private booking link in the invitation email. The page and the email explain that Smile, McLink Group's AI interview assistant, will conduct the interview and that the recruitment team will review your responses. Enter your local mobile number without the country code, choose a date, choose a time, and confirm. Keep the confirmation email, and make sure you are available and in a quiet place at the booked time. If the page says no times are available, reply to your invitation email so the recruitment team can help.

Book a face-to-face interview

If HR invites you to the next step, open the new booking link and choose from the available times. The confirmation shows the date, time, and venue. The page also shows whether the interview is scheduled, completed, or no longer available.

Keep booking links privateThese links are personal and can be used only once. Do not forward them to another person or post them in a group chat. If the link does not work or has expired, contact the recruitment team and ask for a new invitation.
9 · Administration

User accounts, organisations, and settings

These pages are for HR access administrators and settings editors. If you cannot see them in the menu, your account does not have the permission.

User Accounts

Only HR accounts with Review recruitment can manage people. Open User Accounts to see everyone in your organisation, with summary counts of total accounts, active accounts, and settings administrators.

Select “Add user account”.

Organisations (platform administrators)

A McLink platform administrator also sees an Organizations section. Add organization takes a name and a lowercase slug (the slug cannot be changed once created). 10 · Help and good practice

Common questions

“I cannot see a role or applicant.”

Your access may limit you to your own requests, your department, or read-only views. Open Profile to check your permissions, then ask HR to confirm that you have the right access.

“A menu item is missing.”

Menu items appear only when your account has the matching permission (see Who does what?). Ask HR to review your access.

“My request is not moving.”

Check the status and latest comment on the request. It may be waiting for HR to review, returned for more information, or on hold.

Ask Smile (in-portal help)

The portal includes a help assistant called Smile for common questions about how the portal works. Select the Smile button at the bottom right and type your question. Smile explains features and steps, and can tell you your organisation's current Smile Credits balance and, if you work with the pipeline, simple queue and interview counts. Smile does not make hiring decisions, cannot see candidate records, resumes, scores, or calendars, and cannot change anything — so do not enter candidate personal data or passwords. If Smile says it “is currently being configured”, the assistant is not switched on yet; contact HR or the portal administrator for help.

“The role was approved but candidates cannot apply.”

Approval is only one step. HR must complete the Recruitment Setup checklist and publish the role.

“A resume failed.”

Make sure it is a readable, unlocked PDF, DOC, or DOCX file and no larger than 10 MB. Try the retry option. If it still fails, ask HR to check the file or enter the candidate another way. A failed file is not charged a Smile Credit.

“There are not enough Smile Credits.”

The AI action is refused before any work is done — nothing is lost. Buy a credit pack on the Credits page, or ask a user with Manage Smile Credits to add credits. See section 6.

“I paid but the credits are not showing.”

Credits are added only when the payment provider confirms the payment to the portal directly, which is usually seconds but can lag. On the Credits page, select Check payment again. If it has still not appeared after a few minutes, ask a user with Manage Smile Credits or the portal administrator for help, and quote the payment reference shown on the page.

“The calendar shows no times.”

Check the role and interview-type filters, choose the correct month, and refresh. Times run only up to the role's target hiring date. For Face-to-Face Interviews, ask the settings administrator to check the shared HR Google Calendar connection, or add an HR exception slot.

“A candidate says their link does not work.”

Links are personal, single-use, and expire after a few days. If a link has expired or been used, generate a new application link, or for interviews open the applicant record and use Send reschedule link where it is offered.

“The page says someone else changed the record.”

Refresh the page, read the latest information, and then try again. This prevents one person’s changes from replacing another person’s work.

Good practice

Do

  • Use clear, job-related information.
  • Check details before submitting or publishing.
  • Keep candidate information private.
  • Use human judgment alongside screening results.
  • Add a useful comment with every role and applicant decision.

Do not

  • Share private booking links.
  • Use personal accounts for company recruitment work.
  • Make a hiring decision based only on an automated score.
  • Upload a resume to the wrong role.
  • Delete records unless you are sure they should be removed.
Need more help?Take a screenshot of the message, note the role or application number, and contact HR or the portal administrator. Do not include passwords or private booking links.
