# Ella Recruitment Portal — UAT Test Cases

**Project:** Ella Recruitment Portal — Pilot
**Prepared:** 18 September 2026
**Purpose:** Execution-ready user acceptance tests for the complete recruitment journey from internal login through HR interview scheduling, plus supporting administration, security, integrations, credits, notifications, and resilience coverage.
**Status values:** `Not Run`, `Pass`, `Fail`, `Blocked`, `N/A`
**Current pilot intake cap:** 6 resume files per submission for local upload, Google Drive, and OneDrive. Older project documents that mention 8 files are historical and are not the acceptance baseline for this pack.

## 1. UAT objective and acceptance boundary

UAT is passed when:

1. The critical golden path in §5 completes with no Sev-1 or Sev-2 defects.
2. All High-priority cases pass, or have an explicitly approved risk acceptance.
3. Candidate-facing links, tokens, resumes, transcripts, calendar details, and internal records are visible only to their intended audience.
4. Every material role, applicant, booking, credit, and notification transition is persisted with an audit trail and does not silently succeed after a partial failure.
5. The final HR interview is visibly scheduled in the applicant record, Bookings page, and configured shared HR calendar.

This document is a business-facing UAT pack. Automated contract tests remain useful evidence, but a passing automated test does not replace the user action and evidence requested below.

## 2. Scope

### In scope

- Google sign-in, session management, logout, protected routes, and safe redirects.
- Dashboard, role requests, role review, status actions, Recruitment Setup, publishing, and archival.
- Public roles, private candidate application links, candidate applications, resume parsing, and HR manual intake.
- Local, Google Drive, and OneDrive resume intake where the relevant connector is enabled.
- AI screening result review, human HR decisions, Ella Credits, and idempotency.
- AI voice interview invitation, booking, scheduling, execution status, result review, retry, and billing outcomes.
- Final HR interview invitation, token validation, calendar conflict handling, booking, notification, and audit history.
- Applicants, Bookings, Settings, User Accounts, Profile, help bot, notifications, responsive UI, and data isolation.

### Out of scope or environment-dependent

- Provider-specific delivery SLAs outside the portal's control.
- Real candidate hiring decisions or employment offers.
- A live AI voice call when the Pilot is configured in dry-run mode. In that mode, execute the dispatch contract and verify the no-call guard instead of placing a real call.
- OneDrive cases when Microsoft Drive variables and OAuth are intentionally disabled. Mark those cases `N/A` with evidence of the configuration decision.

## 4. Execution conventions

For each case, record:

- tester, date/time, browser, environment URL, account alias, and test data identifiers;
- screenshots or screen recording for user-visible results;
- application ID, role ID, booking slot ID, token state, calendar event ID, queue ID, and credit ledger entry where applicable;
- exact error text for negative cases;
- links to logs, n8n execution IDs, email message IDs, or calendar evidence only in the restricted UAT evidence store.

Do not paste real passwords, OAuth tokens, session cookies, provider secrets, resume contents, transcripts, or unnecessary candidate personal data into this document.

## 5. Critical golden path — login to HR interview scheduled

**Scenario ID:** `UAT-E2E-001`
**Priority:** Critical
**Actors:** `UAT-CREATOR`, `UAT-HR`, `UAT-CANDIDATE-001`, configured n8n/provider/calendar services
**Outcome:** A fresh applicant completes the recruitment path and receives a scheduled final HR interview.

### Preconditions

- The environment is deployed over HTTPS and points to the intended UAT database/tenant.
- `UAT-CREATOR` is active in the portal directory and can create roles.
- `UAT-HR` is active with recruitment review access and can manage applicants and bookings.
- The candidate application and screening workflows are enabled.
- At least 1 CV-analysis credit and the configured voice-interview credit reserve are available.
- The shared HR calendar is connected and contains at least one free final-interview slot.
- A future weekday voice-booking slot is available.

### Steps and expected results

1. Open the portal login page. Verify the portal branding, directory-access notice, and Google sign-in control render without horizontal scrolling. **Expected:** Login is available and no internal data is shown.
2. Sign in as `UAT-CREATOR` with a verified active Google account. **Expected:** A secure session is created and the user lands on the dashboard or the safe requested page.
3. Open Dashboard and Role Requests. **Expected:** The creator sees only the permitted navigation and own-scope metrics.
4. Choose **Create Role Request** and create a staff-addition request for the UAT role with department, salary band, target date, job description, screening criteria, and initial interview questions. **Expected:** Required-field validation is clear; requester identity is taken from the session, not editable form data.
5. Submit the role request. **Expected:** A unique role ID is returned, the role enters the configured HR review status, and the role/status history contains the creator and timestamp.
6. Sign out. **Expected:** The session is cleared and protected pages no longer open.
7. Sign in as `UAT-HR`, open Role Requests, and open the new role. **Expected:** HR can view the role and its status history.
8. Approve or advance the role according to the configured status flow, entering comments where required. **Expected:** The action is accepted once, appears in history, and the role becomes eligible for Recruitment Setup.
9. Open Recruitment Setup. Save a draft with the minimum required job description, screening criteria, and interview questions. **Expected:** The draft saves without publishing the role.
10. Complete the required setup, including the resolved AI interview prompt, posting channel, salary, experience, license and HOD choices where applicable. Validate readiness, then publish the role. **Expected:** Incomplete stages are blocked server-side; a complete role reaches the published state and is available to candidates.
11. Open the public Open Roles page and the published role. **Expected:** The role is visible with the correct job description and apply action; unpublished roles remain absent.
12. As `UAT-CANDIDATE-001`, open the public role page and submit the application with valid candidate details, consent, and a valid resume. **Expected:** A new application ID is created; the candidate receives an acknowledgement or the configured notification is queued; no internal navigation is exposed.
13. As `UAT-HR`, open Resume Screening, select the published role, and verify the candidate/application is visible. If the UAT path uses manual intake, upload the valid resume through the permitted intake path; otherwise wait for the configured screening workflow. **Expected:** The resume is queued and the application is not charged before a successful screening result.
14. Wait for screening completion and open the applicant. **Expected:** The structured AI result, readable strengths/concerns, score/evidence, interview questions, and status history are shown. The result is clearly decision support and not an automatic HR decision.
15. Approve the CV analysis for AI voice interview with a non-empty HR comment. **Expected:** Exactly one voice-invitation work item is queued and the candidate receives a secure expiring booking link through the configured notification path.
16. As the candidate, open the voice booking link. **Expected:** Only valid future weekday ten-minute slots are shown in the candidate's timezone; the token is not exposed in page content beyond its purpose as a booking link.
17. Select a slot and confirm the booking. **Expected:** The slot is rechecked at booking time, the token becomes used, the scheduled instant and timezone are persisted, exactly one call-queue record is created, and a confirmation is queued or sent.
18. As `UAT-HR`, open Bookings and the applicant profile. **Expected:** The voice booking appears once with the correct applicant, role, date, time, timezone, and status.
19. Allow the due voice job to be dispatched, or run the approved UAT worker path. **Expected:** The queue row is claimed atomically before dispatch. In live mode, the provider call ID and outcome are recorded; in dry-run mode, no real call is placed and the dry-run evidence is recorded.
20. Ingest or wait for a terminal voice result. **Expected:** The transcript/structured result is attached to the correct application, the terminal voice credit is charged once according to the outcome, and duplicate callbacks are harmless.
21. Review the voice evidence as `UAT-HR` and approve the applicant for the final HR interview with comments. **Expected:** A final HR booking token/link is generated once, the configured HR venue is retained, and the candidate invitation is queued or sent.
22. As the candidate, open the final HR booking link and select a free shared-calendar slot. **Expected:** Busy/conflicting slots are unavailable; the booking is accepted only once and the token becomes used.
23. Verify the final booking in the applicant profile, Bookings page, notification evidence, and shared HR Google Calendar. **Expected:** The applicant shows final HR interview status `Interview Scheduled`, with correct date, time, timezone, venue, calendar event ID, candidate attendee, and audit history.

### Golden-path pass criteria

- No duplicate role, application, booking, queue row, notification event, calendar event, or credit charge is created by refresh or safe retry.
- The CV charge is exactly one credit after successful screening.
- The voice charge matches the terminal outcome and is applied once.
- Candidate and internal emails contain the configured AI disclaimer where required.
- The final HR interview is visible in all three authoritative user-facing locations: applicant record, Bookings, and the shared HR calendar.
