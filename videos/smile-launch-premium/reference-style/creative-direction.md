# Smile Recruitment Portal — Reference Style

## Visual reference

Hera, Your AI Motion Designer (Demo): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lwnxr6VW4B8

The Smile version borrows the reference's warm grid canvas, restrained pastel glow, crisp prompt-like captions, floating preview cards, cursor and selection details, and gallery pullback. Smile's original logo, product wording, music, and demo UI remain in use. No Hera assets or music are included.

## Motion and pacing

- Seven scenes over 25 seconds: brand, role setup, screening, interview choice, HR review, connected workflow, and CTA.
- Scene entrances use short upward fades; labels type on; the screening panel receives a moving focus outline and cursor; interview choices highlight in sequence; the gallery pulls back to reveal the complete flow.
- Landscape and portrait are composed separately for their frame sizes.
- Final CTA: **Try Smile now**.

## Product accuracy

The score and applicant records visible in the UI plates are illustrative synthetic demo data. Google Calendar availability is described as conditional on connection. HR retains the decision; AI assists review. The earlier Avatar duration line is superseded by Version 2 below.

## Version 2 — 2026-10-09

The full cut follows role setup → candidate application or HR resume intake → role-based CV screening → first HR review → one interview format enabled for the role → interview-result review by HR → face-to-face scheduling after HR approval. Voice is a booked 10-minute call; Live Avatar is a one-time-link interview with recording, camera and microphone consent before start, lasting up to about five minutes. Keep the two HR review gates distinct. The film condenses time and labels all synthetic UI and applicant data.
