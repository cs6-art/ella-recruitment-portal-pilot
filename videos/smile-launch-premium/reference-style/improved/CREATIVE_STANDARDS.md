# Creative standards — Smile Recruitment Portal promo

## Visual direction

- Use the Smile mark, warm off-white canvas, fine grid, restrained coral/lilac wash, and calm high-contrast portal surfaces.
- Let one large product surface own each feature beat. Keep decorative UI subordinate to the task at hand.
- Carry role/applicant identity on a compact route rail. Progress it through the actual workflow; do not imitate a chatbot prompt field because the product is not chat-driven.
- Animate product state and user cause: role brief → drafted criteria; publish → role link ready; CV → evidence; candidate consent → Live Avatar; response → HR review; HR approval → slot choice.
- Prefer transforms/opacity for motion. Use a clear entrance, one state change, and a settled readable hold. Keep route motion seek-safe and deterministic.
- Make a portrait layout a real reflow: role/application cards stack, interview alternatives stack, and route/map content becomes vertical. Do not crop the landscape artboard.

## Product truth

- Smile can draft role details, screening criteria and interview questions from a job description. HR reviews and publishes a public or private link.
- Candidates apply through the role link and see the privacy notice. HR can also batch-upload up to 20 resumes or import from Drive/OneDrive.
- CV evidence and scores support review; they do not make the hiring decision. Label synthetic scores as illustrative.
- HR reviews CV evidence before sending an interview invite.
- Voice and Live Avatar are alternatives enabled by role settings. Do not imply one candidate must complete both. Voice calls are 10 minutes.
- Live Avatar begins after the candidate’s recording consent and camera/microphone check. Use the secure one-time invitation context. Omit its duration until pilot/runtime configuration is reconciled.
- HR reviews the interview outcome, transcript and evidence; the human owns approve/reject and the next step.
- After HR approval, a candidate can select a one-hour face-to-face slot against the configured interviewer/HR calendar. Qualify Google Calendar availability with “when connected.” Do not invent live calendar availability or imply an appointment was booked from a generic slot-selection UI.

## Review standard

- Keep the exact CTA `Try Smile now`.
- Inspect at least one frame per second across the full cut and exact state/cut boundaries; inspect landscape and portrait separately.
- Compare the same timestamps and rubric between generations. Record the source change that explains each score change.
- Before final render, run `npm run check` for each format. After render, verify duration, dimensions, audio stream and full-file decode.
- Do not call the score objective or claim the model learned from the generation. Lessons are project records for future work.
