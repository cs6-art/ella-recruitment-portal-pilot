# Reference comparison

**Reference:** [Hera, Your AI Motion Designer (Demo)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lwnxr6VW4B8), reviewed at 30 seconds.

## What the reference does

- Starts with interaction already underway: a persistent compact prompt bar and moving cursor.
- Keeps one central interface as the focal surface while its contents change in response to the interaction.
- Around 0:10–0:20, develops the prompt into a detailed vertical output; around 0:25, expands that result into a gallery.
- Uses a warm pink grid, crisp white card surfaces, restrained motion, and a result reveal that earns the final gallery.

## Translation for Smile

Smile does not work through a chat prompt, so the promo uses the persistent applicant/route rail as the product-native through-line. The central portal surface changes as the role is drafted, evidence is formed, interview options progress through consent, and HR reviews the result. The final beat resolves to candidate scheduling rather than a six-screen gallery. This carries the reference’s causal motion and evolving central surface without misrepresenting the system.

## Remaining difference

The Smile cut uses several focused product states within one central card, while the reference stays closer to one generated canvas. That is an intentional difference: Smile’s real workflow has distinct role, CV, interview, review, and scheduling screens. A single pretend prompt bar would be visually closer but product-inaccurate.
