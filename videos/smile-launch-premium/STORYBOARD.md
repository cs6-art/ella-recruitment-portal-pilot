## Video direction

Use Smile's actual palette: deep navy `#0E4471`, ink `#172033`, blue `#0071F7`, yellow `#F7CF00`, cool canvas `#F4F7FB`, white surfaces, and blue-gray dividers. Manrope is embedded locally. The visual language moves between a high-contrast navy hook, bright source-faithful product interfaces, and a warm light end card. UI screenshot plates remain visibly marked `ILLUSTRATIVE UI · DEMO DATA` wherever synthetic candidate data appears.

Keep one object moving through the film: a resume sheet becomes a candidate card, its corner becomes an interview choice, and its status line carries into HR review and scheduling. Use continuous push and pan moves with depth, parallax, edge masks, and directional scene handoffs. Motion starts in the first frame, reveals are staggered across each beat, and every frame resolves long enough to read. Let the music pulse at 112 BPM; paper flicks, digital ticks, soft impacts, a low sweep, and a warm CTA confirmation land on visible actions. No voiceover.

The portrait adaptation moves the same candidate journey down a vertical path, stacks the interface panels, and enlarges the key text; it is composed independently at 1080×1920. Keep all essential copy and buttons within comfortable title-safe margins. Never imply automatic hiring, a guaranteed result, or that a candidate must complete both interview types. State plainly that AI supports review and HR decides.
