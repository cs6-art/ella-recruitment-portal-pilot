# Research references

Research focused on short launch films, unusually clear visual hooks, product UI animation, scene continuity, readable motion typography, and sound-led transitions. The source repository and its Brag guidance were inspected before the campaign was designed.

## How the references were reviewed

- Five Brag example MP4s were available in the public repository clone and were sampled visually at multiple points. Brag's `SKILL.md`, supporting guidance, and example source were also read.
- Adobe Firefly's launch film, Canva's Magic Studio film, and Notion's newer AI launch film were opened on their official YouTube channels and visually sampled in the player.
- The Figma Config 2024 AI segment, the 2025 Adobe Firefly film, OpenAI's Sora launch page, and a Contra SaaS UI-motion case page were checked as further launch references. Some of those pages exposed a viewer or metadata but not a playable frame in this session; they are marked below and are not described as watched end-to-end.
- HyperFrames' public launch-source repository was inspected. It lists multiple shipped launch compositions and viewer links. Its viewer pages did not reliably play in this session, so those source examples are supporting references rather than claimed full-video reviews.

## Production decisions from the research

- Start with a human problem expressed in one visual object; reveal the brand only after that idea is clear.
- Preserve continuity by moving the resume/candidate marker between scenes.
- Use staggered type, perspective, crop changes, focus rectangles, and deliberate camera pushes to create motion inside product shots.
- Let authentic product UI prove the claim. Mark synthetic screen data on-screen and keep the watermark visible.
- Keep the visual reading order simple: one headline, one product action, one support detail at a time.
- Use a separate vertical composition rather than trimming the landscape frame.
- Align original music pulses and short sound cues to visible actions, while leaving the story understandable with sound off.
- End with the official Smile mark and the exact CTA: **Try Smile now**.
