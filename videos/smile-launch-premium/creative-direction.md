# Creative direction

## Campaign idea

Tell the portal as one candidate journey. A paper résumé moves from a crowded application pile into a Smile application, then through screening, a candidate-selected interview, HR review, and scheduling. The recurring résumé and moving candidate marker make the workflow feel connected rather than like a stack of feature slides.

The campaign is product-led and editorial: real portal panels anchor the shots; paper, cards, focus rings, cursor movement, status marks, and typography supply the motion around them. The opener poses the workload problem. The close makes the product name and the requested action unmistakable.

## Story and pacing

| Time | Beat | Motion purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:03 | Application pile and workload hook | Paper sheets fan, move, and resolve toward one application. |
| 00:03–00:06 | Smile reveal | The brand and product UI arrive as the application enters the portal. |
| 00:06–00:10 | Resume screening | UI rows reveal in sequence; evidence chips and an illustrative score ring draw on. |
| 00:10–00:15 | Interview choice | Voice-call and Live Avatar paths separate; the candidate chooses one, with privacy consent before the avatar start state. |
| 00:15–00:19 | HR review | Evidence and transcript cues appear; the review controls remain available and untouched to show that HR decides. |
| 00:19–00:22 | End-to-end workflow | Five stage cards align as a candidate marker travels along the route. |
| 00:22–00:25 | Smile close | The path resolves into the official Smile mark, headline, and “Try Smile now” CTA. |

No narration is used. The short on-screen phrases and visible interface actions carry the story with audio muted.

## Motion language

- Use clear, continuous movement: paper slides and settles; UI rows cascade; an evidence ring completes; the interview cards open; a cursor focuses a supporting detail; the route line draws while its candidate marker travels; the Smile mark resolves into the end card.
- Give each interface action a visible start and settle. Keep small UI motion subordinate to the one main subject in each scene.
- Use six inter-scene transitions to keep the résumé and candidate marker moving through the full 25-second arc.
- Recompose each scene for portrait. Titles, interface crops, and cards are placed for a tall screen instead of being cut out of the landscape frame.

## Visual system

The palette follows the Smile identity visible in the portal: deep navy #0E4471, ink #172033, blue #0071F7, yellow #F7CF00, cool canvas #F4F7FB, and white. Manrope is embedded locally. UI panels are cropped from the portal and carry the visible ILLUSTRATIVE UI · DEMO DATA mark where sample candidate information appears.

The animated details are native, editable HTML/CSS/SVG and GSAP elements. This keeps timing and copy easy to revise while the portal panels remain faithful to the real interface.

## Sound

An original locally synthesized pulse-led score supports the sequence. Short designed cues align paper movement, UI actions, interview choice, transitions, and the CTA. There is no external commercial music or generated paid-API media.
