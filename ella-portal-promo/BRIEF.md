## Intent

Create a premium, cinematic product advertisement for the Ella Recruitment Portal Pilot. This is a marketing ad, not a tutorial. Show the verified workflow from resume intake through AI interviews to HR review, and make clear that people make hiring decisions. The request supplied a 20–25 second target and a five-beat story; use the 25-second, 16:9 version.

The repository calls the application the Smile Recruitment Portal and describes Smile as its AI assistant. Keep those source names in reconstructed app UI while using the requested Ella Recruitment Portal Pilot name for the campaign identity.

## Assets

- `assets/smile-recruitment-portal-logo.png` — existing portal mark from `public/smile-recruitment-portal-logo.png`; use in the app shell and closing lockup.
- `assets/ella-promo-score.wav` — original, deterministic synth score generated by `scripts/create-score.mjs`; no samples or third-party music.
- `assets/gsap.min.js` — local copy of GSAP 3.14.2 used for seekable motion.

## Customizations

- Reconstruct only UI elements supported by source pages and components. Each reconstructed screen is labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE UI`; candidate names, role labels, and example scores are fictional demo data.
- Show resume upload/import, role-based screening, phone or Live Avatar interview choices, AI screening/interview evidence, and an HR-controlled decision.
- Use kinetic typography and an original upbeat music bed; no narration or paid generation services.
