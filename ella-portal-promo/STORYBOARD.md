## Video direction

Use the portal palette from `frame.md`: navy and blue for primary hierarchy, light neutral surfaces for the reconstructed application, and the existing yellow accent in small, source-accurate UI details. The outer canvas stays warm and light. Keep app chrome close to the source: white sidebar, pale gray workspace, navy headings, blue actions, and thin blue-gray dividers.

Motion is deliberate and continuous: the opening title rises in two beats as a resume stack fans open; the screening panel sweeps across the drop zone while sample rows arrive in sequence; the interview choices spring in one at a time; evidence bars draw and the fictional demo score counts up; the closing headline lands word by word before the CTA pulses once and settles. Product copy remains readable during every move. Avoid floating bokeh, purple AI gradients, invented metrics, stock photography, and any implication that AI makes the hiring decision.

`ILLUSTRATIVE UI` stays visible on every reconstructed product screen. Demo-only profile values are labeled and fictional. The last card states that HR remains in control.
