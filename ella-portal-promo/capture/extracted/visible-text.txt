Verified product identity
- README calls the application the McLink Recruitment Portal (Smile).
- The portal describes Smile as its AI assistant.
- This campaign uses the requested name “Ella Recruitment Portal Pilot”; the reconstructed app chrome keeps the source name “Smile Recruitment Portal.”

Verified workflow
- HR creates and publishes a role with screening criteria and interview questions.
- Resumes can be uploaded in batches (up to 20), imported from Google Drive or OneDrive, submitted through an application link, or screened individually.
- Smile screens resumes against role criteria. Applicant review exposes the score, recommendation, summary, strengths, gaps, and the role settings applied.
- Depending on role settings, candidates can book an AI voice interview, take a Live Avatar interview, or choose between available formats.
- Interview results and evidence are reviewed by HR. The portal does not make the final hiring decision.
- The applicant profile includes AI screening evidence, interview review, transcript, and status tracking.
